# Project scope

## Research question

How do the structural characteristics of symbolic music generated 
by the released EAM-spectral and EAM-relative checkpoints compare 
with those generated by a baseline GPT-2 model?

## Models compared

- GPT2: vanilla GPT-2 baseline
- GPT2-RE: GPT-2 with randomly initialized structural embeddings
- GPT2-SE: GPT-2 with sinusoidally initialized part and time embeddings

## Inputs and outputs

The models receive controlled symbolic-music prompts and generate 
token sequences that continue those prompts. The generated sequences 
are converted to MIDI files for playback and qualitative inspection.

## Evaluation

The generated music will be compared using:

* Structure indicator (SI)
* Chord progression variation ratio (CPVR)
* Chord progression irregularity (CPI)
* Selected MIDI examples for qualitative illustration and auditory inspection; 
no formal human-listener evaluation will be conducted in the core version.

The metrics will be interpreted as measures of particular structural 
characteristics, rather than as complete measures of musical quality.

## Out of scope

The core version will not include fitness scape plots, other longer-range 
structural analyses, model training or fine-tuning, or a complete reproduction 
of every experiment reported in the EAM paper. These may be considered as 
extensions after the core analysis is complete.
